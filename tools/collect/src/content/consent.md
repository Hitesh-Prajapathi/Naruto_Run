# Hand-gesture data collection: consent

Thank you for helping. Please read this before you continue. (Project owner: edit this file to change the wording; the app stores a hash of the text each subject accepted.)

## Purpose
Your recordings will be used only to **train and evaluate a hand-gesture recognizer** (a computer-vision model that recognises a small set of hand signs for a game).

## What is recorded
- **Webcam video of your hands and upper body** while you follow on-screen prompts (about 10 to 15 minutes).
- **No audio** is recorded. The microphone is never requested.
- A few technical details: your device and browser, camera resolution and frame rate, and the answers you give on the next screen (lighting, background, distance, dominant hand, and optionally a self-reported skin-tone bucket).
- **No name, email or account** is collected. You are given a random **subject ID** instead.

## Retention
Recordings are kept for the duration of the project and are stored in a private location accessible only to the project team. They are not published, sold or shared outside the team. Only aggregated, non-identifying statistics (for example "number of subjects") may appear in reports.

## Your rights
- Taking part is **voluntary**. You can stop at any time.
- **You can withdraw and have your data deleted at any time.** Send your **subject ID** (shown to you on the next screen and again at the end) to the project owner and everything recorded under that ID will be deleted. Keep a note of it: it is the only way we can find your data, because we do not know who you are.
- You can also pause or retake any clip during the session.

By ticking the box you confirm you are 18 or older, have read this, and agree to the recording and use described above.
